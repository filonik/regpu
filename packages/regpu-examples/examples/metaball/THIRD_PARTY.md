# regl

This example ports [`metaball.js`](https://github.com/mikolalysenko/regl/blob/gh-pages/example/metaball.js).
`main.ts`, `background.wgsl`, and `render.wgsl` are adapted from regl,
with the GLSL shaders translated to WGSL.

The `textures/spheretexture.jpg` and `textures/normaltexture.jpg` images are
copied from the upstream
[`example/assets`](https://github.com/mikolalysenko/regl/tree/gh-pages/example/assets)
directory. The upstream example also credits
[Bumpy Metaballs](https://www.clicktorelease.com/code/bumpy-metaballs)
for ideas and code.
